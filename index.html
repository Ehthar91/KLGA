Adaptive KLGA Assessment

A polished, teacher-friendly benchmark platform for Karen language assessment. Students begin around Level 2 and move up or down based on performance.

Level 1FoundationsAlphabet Recognition • Letter Sounds • Vowel Recognition
Level 2Sound BuildingAlphabet + Vowel • Tone Recognition • Blend Sound Recognition
Level 3Blend ReadingAlphabet + Blend
Level 4Advanced Sound BuildingAlphabet + Blend + Vowel
Level 5Phrase ReadingRead and identify complete Karen phrases.

Students begin at Level 2. The test moves up or down based on performance. Levels 1 and 2 include multiple diagnostic skills.
