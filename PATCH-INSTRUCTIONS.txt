KLGA INDIVIDUAL STUDENT TEST CONTROLS PATCH

Replace:
- app.js
- firebase-app.js
- styles.css

New Live Monitor actions:

END TEST
- Stops only the selected student.
- Keeps their saved progress.
- Student can rejoin and resume later.

TERMINATE & RESTART
- Stops only the selected student.
- Permanently clears their saved in-progress attempt.
- Student status becomes Terminated.
- After they rejoin and the teacher approves them again, they start from Question 1.

Safety:
- Both actions show a confirmation popup before taking effect.
- Terminate & Restart is visually marked as the destructive option.

No Firestore rules changes are required.
