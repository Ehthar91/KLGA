AUTOMATIC SPEED REVIEW
KLGA pauses a session student only after a sustained unusually-fast pattern:
- at least 8 questions answered
- 6 of the latest 8 under 2.5 seconds
- average of latest 8 under 3.0 seconds

When flagged:
- student is paused automatically
- progress is saved
- teacher chooses:
  • Resume Test
  • Restart from Question 1

Resume resets the recent pace window so the student is evaluated on a fresh pattern.
Restart clears the saved attempt.

This is a review heuristic, not a cheating determination.

No Firestore rules changes are required.
